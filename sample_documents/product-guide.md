# CloudBox Product Guide

## Overview

CloudBox is a file storage service for small teams. It allows users to upload, organize, share, and restore files from a browser.

## Storage plans

The Starter plan includes 100 GB of storage and supports up to five users. The Team plan includes 1 TB of storage and supports up to fifty users.

## File recovery

Deleted files remain in the trash for thirty days. After thirty days, files are permanently removed and cannot be restored through the dashboard.

## Sharing

Users can share files with specific email addresses or create links. Link sharing can be restricted to members of the organization.
