# Acme Company Handbook

## Working hours

The standard working day is Monday through Friday, from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Employees may work remotely on Tuesdays and Thursdays with manager approval.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive  twenty paid leave days per calendar year. Leave requests should be submitted at least five working days before the planned absence.

## Equipment

New employees receive a laptop, charger, headset, and security key during onboarding. Equipment problems should be reported to the IT team through the internal support portal.

## Communication

The company uses email for formal announcements and Mattermost for everyday team communication. Urgent production incidents should be reported in the incident-response channel.
